# Advanced Analytics Engine Logic

## 1. Overview
The Analytics Engine transforms raw transactional data (Bills, Queue, Staff, Inventory) into actionable business intelligence. It is designed to provide the Salon Owner with a "Command Center" view of the business.

## 2. Revenue Analytics (The Money)
Focuses on financial health and growth.
- **Revenue Segmentation**: Split between Service Revenue and Product Revenue.
- **Average Ticket Value (ATV)**: `Total Revenue / Total Bills`.
- **Revenue Trends**: Time-series analysis (Day-over-Day, Month-over-Month) to identify growth or decline.
- **High-Value Services**: Identification of "Cash Cows" (Services with highest total revenue).

## 3. Operational Analytics (The Efficiency)
Focuses on the "Flow" of the salon.
- **Queue Performance**:
  - **Average Wait Time**: Time from `created_at` to `actual_start_time`.
  - **Service Duration Variance**: Difference between `estimated_duration` and `actual_duration`.
- **Stylist Utilization**: `(Total Time in IN_PROGRESS / Total Shift Hours) * 100`.
- **Peak Hour Heatmap**: Analysis of bill counts grouped by hour of the day and day of the week.

## 4. Customer Intelligence (The Behavior)
Focuses on retention and acquisition.
- **Retention Rate**: `% of customers with > 1 visit`.
- **Churn Alert**: Identifying customers who haven't visited within their usual frequency.
- **CLV (Customer Lifetime Value)**: Total spend per customer since their first visit.
- **VIP Segmentation**: Ranking customers by total spend and frequency.

## 5. Staff Performance (The Human Capital)
Focuses on productivity and quality.
- **Revenue per Stylist**: Total revenue generated by services performed.
- **Customer Loyalty**: Count of "Repeat Customers" specifically for a stylist.
- **Efficiency Score**: Ratio of revenue generated vs. time spent working.
